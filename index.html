01 · The problem

Agent safety today is per-tool and per-person.

Each developer sets their own rules. Nothing is shared across the team, and nothing is tracked across tools.

  1. 01

    No shared team memory

    What one person's agent learns stays with that person.

  2. 02

    No team policy

    Rules live in individual setups, one developer at a time.

  3. 03

    No cross-tool audit log

    There is no single record of what agents did, across tools.

02 · How it works

Every agent write passes guard checks.

Juggr sits between your agents and your work, with three layers that apply to everyone on the team.

Memory

Shared knowledge bases, called Brains, for a person, a team or the whole company, so context isn't trapped in one setup.

Guardrails

  • Every agent write passes guard checks
  • Guard hooks block writes to protected paths
  • Secrets are scanned

Audit log

One record of what agents did, plus approval gates on anything risky.

03 · Approvals and Atlas

Atlas asks before anything high-impact.

Atlas is Juggr's assistant. It runs locally, with daily and weekly loops, and puts a human in charge of the actions that matter.

Approval before high-impact actions

Risky actions wait for a yes. Nothing high-impact goes out on its own.

Runs locally

Atlas runs on your machine, on daily and weekly loops.

Financial actions stay suggest-only

Atlas can suggest. It does not act on money.

Autonomy levels

You choose how much an agent can do on its own, from L0 to L3.

  1. L0least autonomy
  2. L1
  3. L2
  4. L3most autonomy

Always Financial actions: suggest-only at every level.

04 · Team brains

Knowledge moves up only with approval.

A Brain is a shared knowledge base for a person, a team or a company. Knowledge is promoted step by step, never silently.

  1. brain:you

    Draft

    Knowledge starts in a personal Brain.

  2. →
  3. needs approval

    Review

    A person approves before it moves up.

  4. →
  5. brain:team

    Publish

    Approved knowledge reaches the team or company Brain.

In progress Multi-user sharing.
